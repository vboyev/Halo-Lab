FAQ

How long does a full website redesign take?

Timelines depend on the size and complexity of your current website, how much needs to change, and whether you’re updating content, structure, or tech. We work in focused sprints with clear milestones, so you’ll always have visibility into progress and launch expectations.

Do you offer a website audit before we start?

Yes, we begin every redesign with a thorough audit. We evaluate structure, usability, design, and performance to identify what’s working, what’s outdated, and where users may be dropping off. This helps us build a focused strategy and avoid unnecessary rework.

Can you help with content and messaging?

Absolutely. We can audit your existing content, refine your messaging, and suggest a structure that better aligns with your product and users. Whether you need light editing or full-scale rewriting, we’ll help make sure your message is clear, persuasive, and consistent across the site.

What platforms or stacks do you develop on?

We work with modern, scalable platforms like Webflow, WordPress, and headless CMS setups. Our developers are also experienced with React, Vue, and custom frontend solutions, so we can align with your tech stack or help you choose the right one for long-term growth.

Will the new site affect our current SEO rankings?

It can — but in a good way. We take SEO seriously and ensure that redirects, metadata, structured content, and performance are handled properly during the redesign. With the right strategy, your site can maintain rankings and even improve them post-launch.

Can we launch in phases or just redesign certain pages?

Yes. If you need a phased rollout or want to focus on high-impact pages first, we can plan the redesign in stages. This approach helps reduce risk, keeps your site live and functional, and lets you start seeing results sooner while we complete the full transformation.
