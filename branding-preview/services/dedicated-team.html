FAQ

How quickly can we start after the initial consultation?

We can have your dedicated team ready to go in as little as 2 weeks so you can start seeing progress without delays.

Can I have direct communication with the dedicated team?

Yes, direct communication is a priority. You’ll work closely with the team through tools like Slack, MS Teas, or your preferred channels, ensuring clear, real-time updates, fast feedback loops, and total transparency throughout the project.

Can I scale my team up or down?

Absolutely. Our dedicated team model is designed for flexibility. You can scale the team up when your workload increases or down when project requirements shift — all without the stress of hiring or letting go of in-house employees.

Do your teams work in my time zone?

Most of our team members are based in Ukraine, but we ensure seamless collaboration no matter where you’re located. If you operate in a different time zone, we’ll find overlapping time slots that allow for effective communication and real-time progress updates.

What industries can benefit from a dedicated team approach?

Our dedicated teams are versatile and experienced across industries like SaaS, fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, education, and more. If your product demands skilled developers, designers, or project managers, this approach will help you scale efficiently and deliver results faster.

Is there a minimum or maximum contract period for a dedicated team?

We offer flexible contract terms to meet your needs. Whether it’s a short-term engagement for a specific project or a long-term partnership to scale your product, we adapt to your timeline and ensure consistent, reliable results throughout.
